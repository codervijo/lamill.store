# LaMill Storefronts

Build a mobile-first website for **LaMill Store** at **lamill.store**. It’s the home for several distinct storefronts. Give each storefront its own landing page and link to every storefront from the homepage.

- **Homepage `/`** — Show three prominent cards: **Kids’ Activities**, **Etsy Shops**, and **Physical Goods**. Give each a short description, a distinct visual, and a clear button. Feature a free printable below the cards. Keep the main navigation consistent across all pages.

- **Kids’ Activities `/kids`** — Create collections for Bible Adventures, Dinosaurs & Space, Trucks & Trains, and Travel & Quiet Time. Include a preview and free download for each available printable. Feature the idea “Put your child in the story” as coming soon until personalization works. Keep downloads free with an optional email signup afterward.

- **Etsy Shops `/shops`** — Give each LaMill Etsy brand its own profile with a short introduction, product previews, and a **Shop on Etsy** button. Make shop names, images, and URLs easy to configure. Hide unconfigured links.

- **Physical Goods `/goods`** — Create a separate catalog for products we’ll sell directly, starting with ideas like printed activity books and party kits. Support product photos, descriptions, prices, and availability. Show unreleased products as coming soon. Add checkout later.

Use the LaMill folded L logo and navy/blue palette. Keep the homepage neutral and polished, with more playful artwork in the kids’ section. Make it easy to add new storefronts and collections from a single configuration. Verify that all homepage cards and navigation links work on mobile and desktop.
